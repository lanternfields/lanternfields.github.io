Getting started (5 minutes)

  1. Install Precedent in Jira. During installation, also connect Confluence. A site admin is required, and an org admin if you grant the Teamwork Graph permissions.
  2. Open Apps → Precedent in Jira, or Apps → Decision ledger in Confluence.
  3. Go to Precedent settings (Jira → Apps → Manage apps) and choose which spaces and projects to watch. Then run Backfill existing content to capture decisions from existing pages and issues.
  4. Review the Inbox: confirm real decisions and dismiss the rest. Confirmed decisions power the conflict checks.
  5. Open any Jira issue. The Precedent panel in the issue sidebar shows the decisions that apply and any conflicts.
  6. In Rovo, pick Decision Keeper and ask: "What did we decide about our event bus?"

How decisions are captured

Where How to write it
Confluence decision element (/decision) Captured with high confidence and auto-confirmed by default
DACI template The Outcome, Driver, Approver, Status and Due date fields are read
Meeting notes List items under a heading such as Decisions or Outcomes
Any page, issue or comment Phrases such as "we decided…", "we agreed…", "we're going with X over Y because…", "Decision: …"

Questions, proposals ("should we…", "I propose…") and action items are ignored.

FAQ

A decision I can see in Confluence is missing. Check that its space is in scope (Settings → Confluence spaces). Then open the page's Decisions byline item and choose Scan page now.

Why can't a colleague see a decision? Decisions inherit the permissions of their sources. If they can't open the source page or issue, they won't see the decision.

Does Precedent send my data anywhere? No. It runs on Atlassian Forge with no external calls. AI extraction uses Atlassian-hosted models, and admins can turn it off.

The Teamwork Graph section says "not enabled". Teamwork Graph enrichment needs the app's Teamwork Graph permissions, granted by an org admin under Connected apps. Everything else works without it.

How do I connect Claude, Cursor or VS Code (MCP)? See mcp.md.

How do I remove all data? Go to Settings → Data & privacy → Delete all data. Uninstalling also removes app storage.
