Precedent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Precedent ("the app") is an Atlassian Forge app published by Lanternfield Studio ("we"). This policy explains what data the app processes and how.

Summary

  • Precedent runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform ("Runs on Atlassian"). We operate no servers, and the app makes no calls outside Atlassian.
  • Data stays in your Atlassian site's Forge app storage, in your site's data residency location.
  • We, the publisher, cannot access your data.

The app does not collect email addresses, passwords, payment information, or analytics or tracking data.

AI processing

When an admin has enabled "Use Atlassian-hosted AI", text from pages and issues is sent to Forge LLMs. These are Anthropic Claude models hosted by Atlassian inside the Atlassian platform. That text is used only to extract decisions. Requests pass through Atlassian's AI moderation and Acceptable Use Policy. No data is sent to any third party, and the app does not train any model. Admins can disable AI at any time, and the rule-based extractor keeps working.

Access control

Users see a decision only if they can open at least one of its source pages or issues. The app checks this as the viewing user and hides decisions when it cannot confirm access. Decisions recorded manually or through Rovo are visible to users who can use the app on the site.

Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept until it is deleted. Admins can delete every Precedent record from Precedent settings → Delete all data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage according to Atlassian's Forge data-deletion policies.
  • Personal data: the app stores Atlassian account IDs. Every week it reports them through Forge's personal data reporting API. When an account is closed, the app removes that account ID and its cached display name from every decision record (owner, participants, contributors, audit history) and shows "Former user" instead. Cached names of updated accounts are refreshed.

Sub-processors

Atlassian (Forge hosting, storage and Forge LLMs). No others.
