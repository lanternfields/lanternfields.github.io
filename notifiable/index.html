Notifiable

Regulatory incident reporting for Jira Service Management. When an incident might be reportable, Notifiable starts the legal clock at detection. It classifies the incident under DORA with evidence for every criterion, and tracks every regulator's deadline (DORA, GDPR Art. 33, NIS2). It drafts the initial, intermediate and final reports field by field, and keeps a tamper-evident audit trail.
