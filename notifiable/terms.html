1. The App

Notifiable helps teams manage regulatory incident reporting in Jira Service Management. It:

  • assesses incidents against reporting rules (DORA, GDPR Art. 33, NIS2 Art. 23);
  • tracks reporting deadlines;
  • drafts report content;
  • records the classifications and filings that your people make.

The App runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. We operate no servers and receive no copy of your data.

5. AI features

If an administrator enables AI, the App sends incident text to Atlassian-hosted language models (Forge LLMs). The App uses them to extract impact facts and to draft narrative report fields. AI output can be wrong. The App discards AI output that does not quote the incident, and it marks AI-drafted fields. Review every value before you file a report.
