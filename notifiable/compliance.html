Compliance notes: what Notifiable implements and what it doesn't

Notifiable is decision support for people who are accountable for regulatory incident reporting. A person makes each classification and each filing. This page lists what the rules engine covers, with the sources behind it, so a compliance function can review it.

Known limits (deliberately not automated)

  • Mandatory and conditional flags follow our reading of the Annex II glossary. Authority portals, such as the Central Bank of Ireland's xlsx template, may validate more strictly. The exports are inputs to those portals; they do not replace them.
  • Thresholds measured over time (for example "clients affected") use the figures the incident states or a person enters. Estimates are flagged, as field 3.12 requires.
  • NIS2 significance is approximated from the same impact facts. The sector thresholds in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690 are not encoded.
  • Holiday calendars cover nationwide public holidays only (NL, DE, FR, BE, LU, IE) and the TARGET closing days. Admins add regional or bank-specific days themselves.
  • Exemptions: microenterprises and the entities in DORA Art. 16(1) are outside the recurring rule. The app does not detect exempt status; the admin's entity profile decides.
  • No submission: Notifiable never transmits reports to authorities.

Record keeping

Every classification, fact change, draft, filing and reminder is appended to a SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail. It can be verified in the UI and exported as JSON. This supports DORA Art. 17(3) (recording ICT-related incidents) and GDPR Art. 33(5) (documenting breaches).
