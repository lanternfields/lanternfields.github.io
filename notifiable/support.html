The 3-minute path (judges and first-time users)

You need a site with Jira Service Management and Rovo.

  1. Install Notifiable.

  2. Open Apps → Manage apps → Notifiable → Configure → Data → Load sample data. This loads a fictional payment institution's profile, its critical-services register and seven sample cases.

  3. Still on Data, choose Create demo incident. Notifiable creates a real Incident in your service project and assesses it straight away. Or create an Incident yourself and paste this description:

    The card acquiring gateway is failing: roughly 18% of card authorisations are declined.
About 9,800 merchants affected so far. Merchants in NL, BE and DE are affected.
Approx. 41,000 card transactions declined so far; value of the affected transactions is about €1.3M.
A tech news site has published an article about the outage, and complaints are trending.
No evidence of unauthorised access.

  4. Open the incident. Within about 30 seconds (straight away with the button), the sidebar shows Notifiable · Clock · DORA 23h …. Expand the panel: "Meets major criteria", with the evidence for each criterion.

  5. Choose Review & classify → Classify as major. The ring drops to 4 hours. Then Reports → Draft fills the official template.

  6. Open Apps → Notifiable for the console, and Ask Reporting Officer to try the Rovo agent.

Getting started

  1. Install Notifiable on your Jira site with Jira Service Management. Teamwork Graph features need an org admin to approve the graph scopes.
  2. Open Apps → Manage apps → Notifiable → Configure (Notifiable settings):
    • Entity profile: legal name, LEI, type of financial entity, member state, timezone, NIS2 designation, contacts.
    • Critical services: each service, whether it supports a critical or important function, whether it is authorised, and its baselines (clients, daily transactions, daily value, member states).
    • Regimes & calendar: DORA, GDPR and NIS2, plus the bank-holiday preset.
    • Monitoring: service projects and incident work types. Optionally, Assess existing incidents.
  3. Open any incident: the Notifiable panel (Clock) shows the assessment and the deadline. Open Apps → Notifiable for the console.

Don't have incidents yet? Use Settings → Data → Load sample data to explore with a fictional payment institution.

FAQ

When does the clock start? At detection (awareness; field 2.2). Put a line such as Detected: 2026-10-27 09:12 CET in the incident description, or edit Detected (awareness) on the case. Without one, the time the incident was created is used.

Does Notifiable file reports with my regulator? No. It drafts and exports the reports (JSON, CSV and portal text) and records what you filed, with the authority's reference code.

Why is my incident "potentially major"? Some criteria are still unknown and could tip the result. The Assessment tab lists them. Click Add fact to record what you know.

Why didn't the weekend extension apply? It does not apply to initial and intermediate reports of credit institutions, CCPs, trading venues or NIS2-designated entities, or when your authority disapplied it (RTS 2025/301 Art. 5(5)–(6)). Check your entity profile.

Can I turn AI off? Yes, under Settings → AI & integrations. Extraction and drafting fall back to deterministic rules and templates.

How do I build a JSM queue of reportable incidents? Use JQL such as notifiableMajor = yes AND notifiableNextDue <= endOfDay().

Is the audit trail tamper-proof? It is tamper-evident: each entry's SHA-256 hash includes the previous one, and Verify chain detects edits, deletions and reordering.
