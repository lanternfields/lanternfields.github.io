Notifiable: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Notifiable ("the app") is an Atlassian Forge app published by Lanternfield Studio ("we"). This policy explains what data the app processes and how.

Summary

  • Notifiable runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform ("Runs on Atlassian"). We operate no servers, and the app makes no calls outside Atlassian.
  • Data stays in your site's Forge app storage, in your site's data residency location.
  • We, the publisher, cannot access your data.

The app writes two things to Jira:

  • internal notes (deadline reminders), which customers cannot see;
  • an issue property notifiable (state, regimes, next deadline), which is used for JQL search.

AI processing

When an admin enables "Use Atlassian-hosted AI", incident text is sent to Forge LLMs: Anthropic Claude models hosted by Atlassian inside the Atlassian platform. The text is used only to extract impact facts and draft report narratives. AI output is only kept if its quote appears in the incident and actually states the value. AI facts are shown as suggestions that a person must accept before they affect any assessment. Requests pass through Atlassian's AI moderation and Acceptable Use Policy. No data is sent to third parties, and no model is trained on your data. Admins can disable AI at any time, and the rule-based extractor keeps working.

Access control

Users see a case only when they can open its Jira incident. The app checks this as the viewing user and fails closed. Changing a case requires the same access. Settings, imports, backfill and data deletion require Jira administrator permission.

Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept until deleted. Admins can delete all Notifiable data under Notifiable settings → Data → Delete all Notifiable data.
  • Uninstalling removes Forge storage according to Atlassian's Forge data-deletion policies.
  • Personal data: every week the app reports the account IDs it stores through Forge's personal data reporting API. Closed accounts are erased from case documents and shown as "Former user". Audit-trail entries are hash-chained for integrity. Rather than rewrite them, the app redacts closed accounts when it displays them.

Sub-processors

Atlassian (Forge hosting, storage and Forge LLMs). There are no others.
